TESLA, INC. — Q4 2024 EARNINGS CALL TRANSCRIPT
Date: January 29, 2025
Participants: Elon Musk (CEO), Vaibhav Taneja (CFO), Martin Viecha (IR)

[MODERATOR]: Good afternoon and welcome to Tesla's Q4 2024 earnings call.
I'll now turn it over to Elon Musk, Chief Executive Officer.

[CEO - ELON MUSK]: Thank you. Q4 was a record quarter for Tesla. We delivered
495,000 vehicles globally, a 12% increase year-over-year, and crossed the
milestone of 1.8 million total annual deliveries for 2024. Energy storage
deployments hit an all-time high of 11 GWh in the quarter. Optimus robot
production is ramping; we expect to begin limited commercial deployments in 2025.
Full Self-Driving supervised miles crossed 2 billion this quarter. We are
confident in achieving 20–30% delivery growth in 2025.

[CFO - VAIBHAV TANEJA]: Thank you, Elon. Revenue for Q4 came in at $25.7 billion,
up 15% year-over-year. Automotive gross margin improved to 19.8% from 17.6% in Q3,
driven by lower material costs and improved manufacturing efficiency at Gigafactories.
Operating income was $2.3 billion, representing a 9% operating margin. Free cash
flow for the quarter was $2.0 billion. We ended the year with $29.1 billion in
cash and cash equivalents. Capital expenditure for 2025 is expected to be at
least $10 billion, primarily directed toward Gigafactory Texas expansion,
Dojo supercomputing, and new model development.

[ANALYST - DAN LEVY, BARCLAYS]: Elon, can you speak to the competitive pricing
pressure in China from BYD and how it is affecting margins?

[CEO - ELON MUSK]: Great question. China remains highly competitive. We have
reduced prices selectively in certain trims to maintain volume, and our Gigafactory
Shanghai continues to be the most efficient production facility we have globally.
We believe our software and FSD differentiation will sustain our competitive edge
long-term. However, I want to be transparent — near-term margin pressure in China
is real, and we are monitoring it carefully.

[ANALYST - ADAM JONAS, MORGAN STANLEY]: Could you update us on the timeline
for the next-generation affordable vehicle?

[CEO - ELON MUSK]: We remain on track to begin production of our next-generation,
more affordable vehicle in the first half of 2025. This vehicle is critical to
unlocking our next wave of volume growth and will leverage our unboxed manufacturing
process. We expect it to meaningfully expand our total addressable market.

[CFO - VAIBHAV TANEJA]: I would add that while investments are elevated in 2025,
we expect operating leverage to improve meaningfully in the second half of the year
as the new vehicle ramps and energy storage deployments continue to scale.
We are reiterating guidance for 20–30% vehicle delivery growth in 2025.

[MODERATOR]: Thank you. That concludes today's Q4 2024 earnings call.
A replay will be available on our Investor Relations website.
